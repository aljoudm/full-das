# Decisions of full-das (justification; reference draft for the report)

1. **Single parameter table.** `config/parameters_reviewed.tsv` (copy of the optimization table at f51b223, plus column `value_type` and rows added 22 Nov 2026). `run_optimization` reads it through `src/load_params_fd.m`, which drops the 10th column for the frozen loader (the frozen file stays unchanged). Why: one source for every number.
2. **Frozen copies in `third_party/`** with commits in `third_party/PROVENANCE.md`; the sources are never modified.
3. **Cold zone is not zero.** lambda_cold = 1 to 2 events/day for the whole zone (sensitivity 0.1 to 2); spread over cells by risk x length. Why: the Cold performance must be testable.
4. **Allocation = frozen MILP**, 22 real interdiction crossings (32 sites as a sensitivity); each solved case is saved in `results/` because the MATLAB licence is a trial.
5. **Profiling naming and use of height** (Yomna, 22 Nov): 0.4 m is a *channel spacing*; SNR is computed with her `detection_snr_db`, and the 12 dB threshold comes from simulation (the real value is measured in Phase 0); height is exploratory (+/- 8 cm), used only to exclude a match (difference >= `dh_reid_max` = 25 cm, parameter), never to identify or confirm a person. The primary claim of the DAS is stride length, speed and cadence.
6. **DASViT1D** accuracy (93.5 %, published) is for another device and site (Brno dataset); accuracy on FEBUS is unknown until tested on real data.
7. **Simulated Pd_DAS is a result next to the literature value** (user decision 22 Nov): the library gives 0.977 for intrusion types (`tests/test_stage4.m`) next to the literature Pd_DAS = 0.969 (`zeng2024`). The optimization keeps the literature value. The 0.977 depends on the ASSUMED amplitudes and the ASSUMED ground-coupling spread (log-normal, sigma 0.4): with a lower mean coupling or a larger spread it falls, so it must not replace Pd_DAS until it is calibrated on real recordings (Phase 0).
8. **Maps** show Esri World Imagery (attribution printed on every figure; the Esri terms for this use are still to be confirmed), the named places (Masjid al-Haram, Jamarat, Mina, Muzdalifah, Arafat), the entrance checkpoints by name (Al-Sharaye, Al-Hada, Al-Nawariyah, Al-Shumaisi (new), Al-Shumaisi old (Hudaybiyah)), a scale bar (3 km on the 3D overview, 2 km on the 3D zoom, 10 km on the 2D maps) and a north arrow. Corrected 24 Nov 2026 (it said a 10 km bar and five checkpoints).
9. **Class "other"** (longboard, open/close, plus nothing else): kept as its own class in training; in the simulation it means "non-intrusion, no alarm". The report states how much of it is classified as a threat (user decision 22 Nov).
10. **Manipulation is a threat** (tampering with the fibre or the fence disables the monitoring system): merged with fence/impact into one class "climbing" = interaction with the fence or the fibre. Mapping to our classes: walking, running, vehicle, construction -> digging, fence + manipulation -> climbing, regular -> no_event, longboard + open/close -> other. Class animal has NO training data (PLACEHOLDER). **CANCELLED by decision 12** (manipulation = `tampering`, longboard + open/close = `other`).
11. **Evaluation split** (Yomna + user): leave-one-recording-out, never random windows. The random-window accuracy is also computed to show how much it inflates the published 93.5 %. The split file of DASViT1D (`splits/split_indices.pkl`) is not used.
12. **Class map and splits follow Yomna's protocol (23 Nov).** `config/class_map.yaml` (nothing dropped silently; longboard and open/close are merged into the documented non-alarm class `other`; manipulation = `tampering`, changing decision 10), `config/splits.json` (written once, seed 20261122) and `config/splits_addendum.json` (single-recording diagnostic 80 / gap / 20). Re-identification (`reidentify`) is Yomna's: not started here until her design arrives.
13. **One Pd(d) model for the optimization and the simulation, and the d\* rule (23 Nov 2026).** `results/siting/pd_model.mat` (monotone, isotonic Pd against the total optical distance, per intruder type, from the windows of the frozen engine) is used both to derive d\* and by `das_observe` for the detection decision (private random stream seeded from the event, so the common random numbers are kept). **Rule fixed before the dense run (table row `siting_dstar_rule`):** d\* = the farthest distance up to which the lower 95 % limit of the monotone Pd(d), taken on the **overall Pd with the type shares of the Stage 9 run** (walking 49.6 %, running 19.1 %, vehicle 15.6 %, digging 5.4 %, climbing 10.3 %), stays at or above Pd_DAS = 0.969. **Result: d\* = 14.75 km** (weakest-type rule 13 km, point estimate 15 km, upper limit 17 km, first coarse run 17 km; shown for comparison only). Why: the first Stage 9 gave Pd 95.8 % while the optimization assumed 0.969 at every distance; the cause was the 12-window library with nearest-bin lookup (mix Pd 0.854 at the 15 km bin against 0.975 in the dense run).
14. **N = 6 interrogators (23 Nov 2026).** Analytic minimum N_min = ceil(145.47 / (2 x 14.75)) = 5. The MILP with candidate shelters gives 6 in low, medium and high (same six sites, boundary arcs 8.70, 29.58, 58.44, 85.95, 108.90, 125.19 km): with N = 5 forced the stage-1 coverage term is still violated, 4 cells (about 4.1-4.2 km) stay without DAS; N = 7 is what the weakest-type rule (d\* = 13 km) gives. The slack of N = 5 is only 0.2 km per side (14.75 against 14.55 km). Eight siting sensitivities (slope, road, waterway, inside distance) all give N = 6. The old main model (4 interrogators) is kept for comparison.
15. **Shelter site filters and the checkpoint exemption (23 Nov 2026).** A road-site shelter must stand inside the boundary polygon 50-200 m from the boundary line, within 200 m of a road, on a slope below 10 %, at least 50 m from an OSM waterway, at most one candidate per 300 m of arc (all ASSUMED, table rows `siting_*`). The five entrance checkpoints are always candidates whatever the filters say (row `siting_checkpoint_exempt`): they already have a building, power, access and staff. The Al-Shumaisi (new) checkpoint has a slope of 15.3 % and is used for that reason. Lead-in fibre = 1.4 x the straight distance, added to the cost and to the optical distance.
16. **Shelter cost and cooling (24 Nov 2026).** Every interrogator needs an air-conditioned shelter (5-40 degC). C_shelter = 120,000 SAR per new cabin (PLACEHOLDER: no Saudi price found, needs a quotation); at an entrance checkpoint 30,000 SAR (ASSUMED, existing building). Six sites: 4 checkpoint rooms + 2 new cabins = 360,000 SAR; C_total 13,014,986 SAR. Cooling 170 W per cabin (50 % of the site load, gsma_coolsure) is part of the power: 6 x (170 + 170) = 2,040 W = 7.0 % of P0 = 29,100 W. N and the sites do not change for cabin prices of 50,000-300,000 SAR.
17. **Single verification before a patrol (Stage 6).** Dual verification (a second check by another drone or camera before sending a patrol) was tested as options B (everywhere) and C (Neutral and Cold only) and rejected: in the medium scenario it lowers the false patrols by only 0 to 13 % (0.200 -> 0.199 per km per day) while the patrol reached falls from 87.5 % to 59.6 % and the median verification time rises from 518 s to 1,193 s, because a second mission occupies the scarce drones (busy for the mission and the 27 min charge). The base is the single verification (option A).
18. **The cable is buried (decision D3).** The fibre is buried along the boundary, not mounted on the fence (table row `D3_installation`). It supports the log-normal ground-coupling factor of the DAS window library (sigma 0.4, ASSUMED) and removes the fence-mounted case from the classifier protocol; the real coupling is to be measured in Phase 0.
